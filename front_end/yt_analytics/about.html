About Wilton Moore

▶ Background

/ Profile

MS candidate in Data Science & Business Analytics (GPA 3.75) with 12+ years in the music industry. I’m a Grammy-nominated producer and mixing engineer who now focuses on using Python, SQL, and visual analytics to answer questions about artists, catalogs, and audiences.

I specialize in turning messy music and student data into clear, decision-ready insights for non-technical teams. I love sharing information and insights, especially when it helps artists and teams move with confidence. Selected work includes CatalogLAB, streaming & audience dashboards, and a Student Schedule Optimization Tool that reduced prep time by ~20%. Keywords: music analytics, streaming & catalog data, creator royalties & fair pay, audience segmentation, A/B testing basics, data storytelling & dashboards. I’m looking for early-career roles in music analytics, royalties/rights data, streaming platforms, labels, PROs, publishers, or music-tech startups. Open to remote U.S. roles and summer relocation.
