▶ Background


TrackStats YT starts with YouTube-only data, normalizes it, and translates it into momentum, resonance, and risk signals that can be read in minutes. Each view is grounded in time-series trends, distributions, and simple regression checks so the story stays honest. The goal is to surface patterns, quantify movement, and keep the artist’s context intact.

▶ Decision support


Do: use YouTube API data only, quantify lift and decay, show distributions, and anchor every callout to time-series evidence. If the sample is small, say it. If the signal is weak, say it.


Don’t: claim revenue, CTR, or predictive accuracy without proper validation. Don’t hide limitations. Don’t over-promise. The job is to empower decisions, not prescribe them.
